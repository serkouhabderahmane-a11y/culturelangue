Description

A program designed for real progress

Join a program designed to help you make real progress in a motivating and stimulating environment, specially created for adult learners.

Our small-group classes (maximum of 5 participants) offer far more than a simple course: you benefit from a dynamic space where you can practice, ask questions, and build confidence in every session.

  • Intensive 60-hour Express pathway (4 weeks, Mon–Fri)
  • Flexible 30-hour Evening Lingo pathway (10 weeks, 1x/week)
  • Immersive 30-hour Saturdays in English pathway (5 Saturdays)
  • Small groups of 5 participants maximum
  • Expert guidance from certified instructors
  • Free initial assessment and personalized advice
  • Flexible scheduling to match your availability

With a structured approach centered on your goals, you progress faster and more effectively, while receiving guidance at every step of your learning journey.

Whether you want to improve your skills for work, studies, or everyday life, this program gives you the tools and support you need to reach your goals.
